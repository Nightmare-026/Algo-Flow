## Brand & Style

This design system delivers an authoritative, academic yet universally approachable aesthetic rooted in lifelong learning, prestige, and upward mobility. Balancing the stature of global tier-one universities with the high-velocity ergonomics of modern enterprise digital platforms, the design language communicates institutional trust, rigorous pedagogy, and clear achievement.

The design movement centers on **Corporate / Modern** augmented with crisp editorial clarity and subtle academic rigor. Visuals prioritize extreme readability, structured content modularity, frictionless search-and-discovery mechanics, and proud signaling of achievements (degrees, specializations, verified certificates). Whitespace is generous, chromatic saturation is deliberate and purposeful, and structural borders are whisper-quiet to maintain emphasis on educational media, partner branding, and syllabus hierarchies.

## Colors

The core chromatic strategy pairs the signature Coursera royal blue with deep collegiate navy, warm institutional gold, and verified credential emerald. 

- **Primary (`#0056D2`)**: Drives high-leverage actions, primary buttons, active navigation, active step indicators, and progress tracks.
- **Secondary (`#0A1128`)**: Deep collegiate navy used for high-impact enterprise headers, degree landing page hero zones, and footer containers.
- **Tertiary (`#F2B418`)**: Warm partner gold reserved for prestige flags, ratings, honors distinctions, and institutional highlights.
- **Credential Emerald (`#0F8A5F`)**: Applied to successful course completions, verified identity badges, certificate validation seals, and active enrollment indicators.
- **Neutral Stack**: `#1F1F1F` provides high-contrast, WCAG AAA text contrast on neutral surfaces (`#FFFFFF`, `#F8F9FA`, `#F5F7FA`), while `#5E6977` handles metadata, instructor titles, and timestamps without visual clutter.

## Typography

The type system is powered by Source Sans 3 across all surfaces to reflect clear, accessible instruction and editorial authority.

- **Headlines & Titles**: Display and headline tiers employ medium to bold weights (`600` and `700`) with modest negative tracking for structural tightness on hero banners and course titles.
- **Long-form Reading & Syllabi**: Body text utilizes generous relative line heights (1.5 to 1.55) to support dense syllabus comprehension, transcripts, assessment instructions, and forum threads.
- **Metadata & Data Points**: Micro-labels, difficulty metrics, certificate details, and university partner lines use uppercase or semi-bold labels with deliberate positive tracking (`0.01em` - `0.02em`) to guarantee legibility at small scale.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts adhere to an 8-point rhythmic grid with a 12-column responsive layout system maxing out at `1280px` for optimal reading scan lines.

- **Breakpoints**: 
  - Mobile: `< 600px` (4 columns, `margin-sm` 16px, `gutter-sm` 16px)
  - Tablet: `600px - 1024px` (8 columns, `margin` 24px, `gutter` 24px)
  - Desktop: `> 1024px` (12 columns, `margin` 32px, `gutter` 24px, max content container `1280px`)
- **Spacing Rhythm**: 
  - Internal card padding strictly uses `space-md` (16px) or `space-lg` (24px).
  - Module blocks on syllabus views leverage `space-xl` (40px) to separate learning objectives cleanly.
  - Micro-gaps between partner badges, stars, and course durations strictly rely on `space-xs` (4px) and `space-sm` (8px).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses clean tonal layer separation complemented by precise ambient shadows. Surfaces avoid heavy skeuomorphism in favor of structural clarity:

- **Surface Level 0 (Base Canvas)**: `#FFFFFF` for main catalog content, or `#F8F9FA` for dashboard canvas and lecture transcript containers.
- **Surface Level 1 (Card & Modular Tier)**: `#FFFFFF` bounded by a 1px border of `#E1E6EB`. Ambient resting elevation: `0 1px 3px rgba(10, 17, 40, 0.06), 0 1px 2px rgba(10, 17, 40, 0.04)`.
- **Surface Level 2 (Interactive Hover / Focus State)**: Cards elevate on cursor hover to signify actionability: `0 8px 24px rgba(10, 17, 40, 0.08), 0 2px 6px rgba(10, 17, 40, 0.04)`. Border shifts dynamically to `#C2CBD6`.
- **Surface Level 3 (Drawers, Global Navigation, Sticky Video HUDs)**: Elevated above canvas with `0 12px 32px rgba(10, 17, 40, 0.12)`.

## Shapes

The design system incorporates **Soft (Level 1)** geometric boundaries to deliver an authoritative yet contemporary software experience:

- **Base Radius (`0.25rem` / `4px`)**: Applied to functional inputs, checkboxes, table cells, code blocks, and assessment answer selections.
- **Card & Surface Radius (`0.5rem` / `8px`)**: Standard for course discovery cards, partner cards, degree informational modules, and dialog overlays.
- **Full Pill Radius (`9999px`)**: Exclusively reserved for interactive filter chips, learning category tags, difficulty pills (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced), and credential status pills.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Background `#0056D2`, text `#FFFFFF`, font weight `600`, radius `4px`. Height: `44px` (desktop), `48px` (mobile). Hover: `#00419E`. Focus: 2px offset ring in `#0056D2`.
- **Secondary / Outlined**: Transparent background, 1.5px border `#0056D2`, text `#0056D2`, hover fills with `#EBF3FF`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost**: Transparent background, text `#1F1F1F`, hover fills `#F5F7FA` with no border shift.

### Chips & Pill Filters
- Fully rounded pills (`border-radius: 9999px`) with padding `6px 16px`.
- Unselected: Background `#FFFFFF`, 1px border `#E1E6EB`, text `#1F1F1F`.
- Selected: Background `#0056D2`, border `#0056D2`, text `#FFFFFF`.
- Counter pill nested inside chip: Sized down (`18px` circular), background `#EBF3FF` (unselected) or `#FFFFFF` with blue text (selected).

### Course & Degree Cards
- Background `#FFFFFF`, 1px border `#E1E6EB`, radius `8px`.
- Aspect-ratio thumbnail header (`16:9`) with bottom border `#E1E6EB`.
- Partner logo badge: 24px height image anchored at the card's top-left content boundary.
- Typography: Partner institution label (`caption`, `#5E6977`), course title (`headline-sm`, max 2-line clamp), credential type / degree line (`label-sm`).
- Footer micro-tokens: Rating badge with `#F2B418` icon + bold score (`14px`), total enrolled count, and level indicator tag.

### Micro-Tokens & Badges
- **Difficulty Badges**: Soft background pills (`Beginner`: `#E7F6F0` / `#0F8A5F`; `Intermediate`: `#EBF3FF` / `#0056D2`; `Advanced`: `#FEF7E6` / `#B88100`).
- **Verified Credential Ribbon**: High-visibility banner featuring `#0F8A5F` seal icon with uppercase `label-sm` ("Shareable Certificate" / "University Degree").

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Inputs**: 44px height, `#FFFFFF` background, 1px `#C2CBD6` border, `4px` radius. Focus switches border to `#0056D2` with a `0 0 0 3px rgba(0, 86, 210, 0.15)` focus halo.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: 20px box dimensions, 1.5px `#5E6977` border, filled with `#0056D2` and white check glyph when active.
